EVERY VOTE.

An NFL season played through Survivor rules. Alliances decide who stays, fantasy scores shape the stakes, and every jury vote eventually becomes two players on a championship roster.

The produced show takes us through Harry’s exit at the final seven. After that, this archive follows the raw Tribal recordings, Ponderosa, player confessionals, and the championship box scores through the final NFL matchup.

04 / THE CHAMPIONSHIP

THE FINAL
TWO WEEKS.

The final three were Aly, Navi, and Steve. Jury votes added NFL players to their teams; the two-week fantasy scores determined the winner.

01 / HOW IT WORKED

Each jury vote gave its finalist the voter’s two best fantasy football players for a two-week championship spanning NFL Weeks 14 and 15. Aly collected five votes, Navi three, and Steve none. Those votes built the finalists’ available rosters; the fantasy scores determined the winner.
